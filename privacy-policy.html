Privacy Policy for Language Barrier Breaker

Last updated: October 22, 2026

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Most translation happens entirely on your device.

Internet Usage

The app uses the internet only for:

  • Checking for updates (no personal data sent)
  • Opening donation links (including Venmo) and Discord links in your browser
  • Support chat messages you choose to send
  • Extra online languages — the core 59 languages translate fully on your device and never leave it. If you pick one of the additional online languages, only the text you type or speak for translation is sent to Google Translate and the result comes back. Nothing is stored by us (we have no servers)
  • Street map tiles — if you open Street mode, map images are downloaded from OpenStreetMap for the area on screen, like any map app
  • Referrals — if you enter a friend's share code, the app records that code and a random install ID in a small referral ledger (Firebase) so both 100-coin bonuses can pay automatically. No name, email, or personal information is attached.

Permissions

  • Microphone — voice translation and ambient listening, on-device only
  • Camera — text recognition from photos and the food lens (identifies dishes and shows recipes); photos are processed on-device only and are never uploaded or sent anywhere
  • Notifications — Thursday witch reminder, if enabled
  • Background microphone service — keeps Listen running when the app is in the background
  • Exact alarms — schedules the Thursday witch reminder reliably
  • Internet — update checks, donation/Discord links, support chat (you choose), the extra online languages, and street map tiles
  • Network state — checks if you're online before trying online features
  • Location — only if you open Walk & Earn or Street mode and tap Start. Your distance is measured on your device; your location is never stored, uploaded, or shared with anyone

No audio or images ever leave your device. Translations only leave your device when you choose one of the extra online languages, as described above.
