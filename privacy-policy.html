Privacy Policy for Language Barrier Breaker

Last updated: October 11, 2026

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Translation happens entirely on your device.

Internet Usage

The app uses the internet only for:

  • Checking for updates (no personal data sent)
  • Opening donation/Discord links in your browser
  • Support chat messages you choose to send

Permissions

  • Microphone — voice translation and ambient listening, on-device only
  • Camera — text recognition from photos and the food lens (identifies dishes and shows recipes); photos are processed on-device only and are never uploaded or sent anywhere
  • Notifications — Thursday witch reminder, if enabled
  • Background microphone service — keeps Listen running when the app is in the background
  • Exact alarms — schedules the Thursday witch reminder reliably
  • Internet — update checks, donation/Discord links, support chat (you choose)
  • Network state — checks if you're online before trying online features

No audio, images, or translations leave your device.
